Score the answer from 1 to 5 for each metric.

RELEVANCE:
5 = directly answers the question.
4 = mostly answers it.
3 = partially answers it.
2 = barely related.
1 = irrelevant.

CORRECTNESS:
5 = fully consistent with expected answer and behavior.
4 = mostly correct with a minor issue.
3 = partially correct.
2 = major errors.
1 = substantially incorrect.

FAITHFULNESS:
5 = every factual claim is supported by policy context or tool results.
4 = almost fully supported.
3 = meaningful unsupported claims exist.
2 = large parts are unsupported.
1 = contradicts or ignores the evidence.

HELPFULNESS:
5 = clear, complete and useful.
4 = useful with a minor limitation.
3 = somewhat useful.
2 = minimally useful.
1 = not useful.

HALLUCINATION FREE:
5 = no fabricated or unsupported claims.
4 = insignificant unsupported detail.
3 = some unsupported information.
2 = substantial unsupported information.
1 = heavily fabricated.

Use ONLY the expected answer, expected behavior, policy context and tool results as evidence. Do not use outside knowledge.
